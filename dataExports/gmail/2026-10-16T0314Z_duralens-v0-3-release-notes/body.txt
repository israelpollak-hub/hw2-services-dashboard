DuraLens v0.3 is released. Highlights:
- New convexity view in duralens-dashboard, with bucket drill-down
- Faster pricing: bond-pricer now prices a 2,000-bond portfolio in about 1.2 seconds, down from 4.5
- Treasury curve now comes from yield-curve-builder (Nelson-Siegel)
- bayview-reporting can export the monthly duration report as PDF

Known issues: the YTM solver in bond-pricer fails to converge for a few deeply discounted bonds, and I am working on a fix for v0.4. Please test the Bayview portfolio on v0.3 and report anything odd by Thursday. Open issues in GitHub rather than replying by email so we can track them.
