# bayview-reporting

Generates client reports for the **Bayview Teachers Pension Fund** from **DuraLens** data.

## Owner
Sruly (junior analyst) builds the reports; Priya Natarajan reviews code; Joe Smith approves what goes to the client.

## Reports
- Monthly duration report requested by Rachel Stein (CIO, Bayview)
- Quarterly portfolio review pack for the investment committee
- Stress test summary with +/-100 basis points results from Daniel Okafor

## Contents of the monthly report
- Portfolio duration (currently 6.4 years) and convexity (0.82)
- Duration by bucket versus the benchmark
- Stress test table and limit usage
- Q4 rebalance progress

## Relation to DuraLens
Reads results from duralens-core and stress-test-engine. Charts follow the style of duralens-dashboard. Maria Delgado signs off on the methodology notes.

## Schedule
First report due in November; template draft due Oct 14.

## Rules
Never send a report to the client before Joe Smith approves it.
